Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

1. What We Collect

VoidCode collects minimal data to operate the service. Specifically we may collect your email address if you sign up for the waitlist or create an account, and anonymised usage metrics (e.g. number of deploys) to improve the product. We do not collect or store the code generated in your Claude conversations.

2. What We Do Not Collect

VoidCode does not read, store, or transmit the content of your Claude.ai conversations. Code captured by the Extension is processed locally in your browser and optionally synced to your own GitHub account — it never passes through our servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your email address is used solely to send product updates and launch notifications. We will never sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

VoidCode integrates with GitHub and Vercel at your direction. Any data you choose to push to those services is governed by their respective privacy policies. Payments are processed by LemonSqueezy — we do not store payment card information.

5. Data Storage

Project files you sync to Void Cloud are stored securely using Supabase infrastructure. You can delete your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Cookies

The VoidCode website uses no tracking cookies. The Extension uses Chrome's local storage API to persist your project state locally on your device.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing support@voidcode.dev.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via email.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at support@voidcode.dev.
